Institutions need the same data we built for agents

Traditional finance is moving onchain

Funds, curators and treasury teams need an accurate view of positions, liquidity, yield and risk.

They can use the data today

Research, backtests and allocation decisions need financial context before a customer delegates execution.

Our AI focus still matters

As institutions add AI, the same datasets can support their existing processes and their agents.

As AI, crypto and TradFi converge, custom institutional data is our strongest near-term revenue opportunity.
