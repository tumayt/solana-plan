Pricing Proposal

Proposed tier structures. Let's discuss.

Number of tiers, gating model, and what each tier includes are open for discussion.

Tier Who Access
Free Evaluators Strict limits - enough to test, not build on
Paid Smaller projects TBD - see gating discussion
Premium Serious yield projects, institutions TBD - see gating discussion
Custom Large enterprises SLAs, dedicated support
Key Decision

How do Paid (2nd tier) vs Premium (3rd tier) differ?

A Gate by endpoint

DeFi data (pools, pricing) at Paid (2nd). Yield, curator, and risk data at Premium (3rd).

B Gate by freshness & rate limits

All endpoints at every tier. Premium (3rd) gets faster refresh and higher rate limits.

C Hybrid

e.g., all endpoints available but risk/curator data gets slower refresh at lower tiers.

Open Questions

Pro: ~$150-200 · Institutional: ~$1-2K · Custom: contact us

Do we offer traditional PAYG alongside x402?
x402 (pay-per-call) is staying. Do we also add usage-based subscriptions for customers who want predictable billing?
How do we price for curators?
AUM-scaled, not flat rate. A $2B curator pays differently than a $50M one. Start mid-tier to validate.
