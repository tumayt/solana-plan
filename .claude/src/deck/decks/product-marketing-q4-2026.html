D · Recommendations for Q4 and 2027

Q4 and 2027 goals.

1

Decide whether to market Offchain Intelligence harder. Deep42 leads usage with little promotion.

2

Add product landing pages and clearer docs. Offchain Intelligence first, then Lending.

3

Put live numbers and dashboards on the homepage. It gets 62% of views.

4

Publish more case studies. Follow TrueNorth with our larger customers.

5

Test ads on a small budget. Google, X and LinkedIn, judged by signups and keys.

6

Refine pricing. One tier today, and users hit rate limits.

7

Fix our measurement. Monthly endpoint usage, and dated web analytics.

Goals from the session outline plus two added: decide on Offchain Intelligence marketing (Brian's note) and fix measurement (Grafana export, dated GA with sources, check docs tracking). Pricing at launch: one Max tier at \,500 a month. Rate limits: about 10 to 15% of 4XX errors are 429s (Oct 5 engineering meeting). Offchain Intelligence means Deep42 social data. Case study candidates should be customers in production; Lhava, Franklin Templeton and Arbitrum are not live yet. Pricing page views: 3,035 on /pricing.
